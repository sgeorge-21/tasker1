About TASKER

TASKER is a modern service marketplace that connects people who need work done with skilled professionals who can do it. Whether you need a carpenter, plumber, designer, or any other professional service, TASKER makes it easy to find, hire, and manage professionals all in one place.

Our Mission

To make hiring skilled professionals as simple and trustworthy as possible, enabling people to focus on what matters most while connecting them with quality service providers.

Our Vision

To become the world's leading marketplace for skilled professional services, where trust, quality, and affordability are guaranteed.

Why We Started TASKER

We recognized a gap in the market: finding and hiring skilled professionals was unnecessarily complicated, time-consuming, and risky. We founded TASKER to change that. Now, thousands of users benefit from our platform every day.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to use TASKER?

+

TASKER is free to use for posting tasks and receiving proposals. We take a small service fee (typically 5%) when you make a payment to a professional. There are no hidden fees or subscription requirements.

How do I know if professionals are qualified?

+

All professionals on TASKER are verified through our platform. You can review their profiles, check their experience, see verified work history, and read reviews from previous clients before hiring.

What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

+

We have a dispute resolution process to handle this. If you're not satisfied, you can open a dispute, and our support team will help resolve the issue fairly. Your funds are protected until you're happy with the work.

How do I get paid as a professional?

+

Once a client releases payment (usually after accepting the completed work), the funds are transferred to your account. You can withdraw your earnings to your bank account at any time.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept credit cards, debit cards, bank transfers, and digital wallets like PayPal. Professionals can withdraw earnings via bank transfer or other supported methods in their country.

How quickly can I get a professional?

+

On average, you'll receive proposals within 1-3 hours of posting your task. Urgent tasks are often completed the same day. Response time depends on the service category and task complexity.
